**Chuhai ABC (fwqabc.com)** collects VPS deals, free-server notes, and line explainers so a reader can compare them. We do not sell servers.

## Editorial rules

1. **Do not invent.** Prices, test numbers, and scores have to come from material we have actually seen. Anything missing stays empty or marked unverified.
2. **The merchant site wins on price.** Deals, stock, and end dates follow the merchant page, with the check date in Beijing time.
3. **Mark what is not verified.** Second-hand claims, or items the merchant has not published, keep an unverified mark until they are checked.
4. **Affiliate links do not change those rules.** Some links pay a commission. That does not change your price and is not traded for a conclusion. See the [disclosure](/en/disclosure/).

## How a price is checked

- Prices and offer rules are taken from the merchant site, an official campaign page, a product catalog, or an official notice, with a source and a Beijing-time check date.
- If the only source is a third party, or the merchant has not confirmed it, it stays marked unverified.
- We do not invent prices, test data, or scores. A merchant we have not bought from does not get a performance conclusion.

## How often pages change

- During a sale such as Singles' Day or Black Friday, offer status is checked daily. Other articles are reviewed at least monthly.
- The article shows when it was last updated.
