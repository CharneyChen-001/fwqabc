## United States

LLC and INC filings are available in more than one state. People often ask about Wyoming, Delaware, Nevada, California, Florida, New York, Texas, Washington, Colorado, and Montana. Other states can be asked about. This page does not turn every state into a table.

These are usual points, not promises:

- A US company filing usually applies for a federal EIN at the same time.
- A New York LLC is generally not suggested, because of the publication step. Ask about an INC, or about another state.
- A name that includes words such as foundation, association, or alliance is usually possible in only some states, and has to be checked.
- Some states later have a state tax, a franchise tax, a first annual report, or a publication step. This page does not give figures. The group does.

Related items you can ask about, without a catalog: annual maintenance, a change, a closure, EIN, ITIN, a registered agent, and a mailing address.

## United Kingdom and Europe

- **United Kingdom.** The usual papers are the certificate, the articles, and the registers of directors and members. The registry identity check is done online.
- **France.** A filing often involves the registration papers, a registered address, VAT, and EORI. What is actually included is confirmed when you ask.
- **Romania.** A filing often involves the registration extract, the articles, a registered address, VAT, and EORI. What is actually included is confirmed when you ask.

## Canada

Ordinary companies in Ontario and British Columbia, and a federal company, can all be asked about. Which one fits is confirmed in the group from what you need it for.

## Hong Kong, Singapore, Korea, Malaysia, and Macau

- **Hong Kong.** Company registration, plus annual maintenance and an address change.
- **Singapore.** A local director, a company secretary, and a registered address are usually required.
- **Korea, Malaysia, and Macau.** Company registration can be asked about. The documents follow the local rules explained in the group.

## Related items

Directions only, not a product list:

- Tax numbers: EIN and ITIN.
- Address and agent: registered agent and a mailing address.
- Banking: some business or personal accounts can be asked about. This page does not name a bank. Some routes pause. Ask what is open at the time.
- US company tax filing, and sales permits.

## Documents to prepare

The website does not collect files. These are the papers people usually need:

- A proposed company name in English
- A photo of a valid ID, such as a passport, a national ID card, or a driver's license
- The address on that ID
- A phone number and an email address
- An SSN or ITIN, if you already have one
