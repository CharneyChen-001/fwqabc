**CN2** is China Telecom's next-generation bearer network, autonomous system **AS4809** ([ipinfo.io/AS4809](https://ipinfo.io/AS4809)). **CN2 GIA**, as merchants use the name, usually means both directions stay on CN2. It is usually more expensive than an ordinary international line and is described here as fitting sites **whose visitors are mainly in China** and that care about evening-peak delay and loss.

- **Fits:** trade or company sites aimed at visitors in China, and admin access that needs to stay up.
- **Often unnecessary:** if visitors are mainly outside China, an ordinary international line or BGP is often enough and much cheaper.
- Behavior depends on the data center, the time, and where you are. This site does not give a speed conclusion before its own test.
