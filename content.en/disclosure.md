## Commission

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, the merchant may pay us a commission. Your price does not increase because of that. Commission helps pay for servers, test machines, and the domain.

## How links are marked

Affiliate links go through `/go/…` and use `rel="sponsored nofollow"`. An article that contains one says so at the bottom.

## Tests

- If a review is published, its numbers come from a real test machine. They are not invented or edited.
- Commission does not change the conclusion. Limits and drawbacks stay in the text.
- Prices and offers follow the merchant site, and the article states when they were checked.

## Where a test machine came from

A review states whether the machine was **bought by us**, **sponsored by the merchant**, or from another source. A sponsored machine is still tested under the rules above. The merchant does not get to review or change the conclusion.
